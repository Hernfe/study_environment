# NBE-E4210 Structure and Operation of the Human Brain

Course-specific section. The course's full rules live in the repo, which
wins over this file:

- `CLAUDE.md`: source hierarchy, reading the material, the lecture build
  workflow, content, writing and design rules.
- `docs/exam-format.md`: the mini-exam format, from Mini Exam 1 with
  solutions.
- `docs/scope/nbe-e4210/L0X.md`: per-lecture scope files, with the
  notes-to-deck slide mappings.

Summary:

- Slug: `nbe-e4210`. Aalto University, autumn 2026, running now.
  Instructor Matias Palva.
- 11 lectures (schedule in `src/content/nbe-e4210/registry.js`, from the
  most recent deck). L01 to L04 built.
- Assessment: weekly one-hour paper mini-exams, about two weeks after
  each lecture, one handwritten A4 cheat sheet allowed. Multiple choice,
  scenario multiple choice, classification, ranking, matching and
  completion from word banks, figure labelling from a word bank, true or
  false with one-line corrections. 1 point per single answer, 0.25 per
  sub-item, 10 points per paper. No essays, no calculations.
- Consequences for the site: recap cards are cheat-sheet drafts; the
  lecture quiz follows the mini-exam mix enforced by the lint; medium is
  the paper's ceiling, hard deliberately above it; essays only as
  `beyondExam`.
- Sources in `source/nbe-e4210/`: slides (scope), TA notes reviewed by
  the teacher (order, objectives, self-checks, textbook page filter),
  Bear, Connors and Paradiso 4th ed. in two files (depth; page offsets
  in `CLAUDE.md`), mini-exams with solutions and two pre-2020 essay
  papers (`exams/`), homework (answer style; also scope).
- Profile: conceptual and anatomical, lightly quantitative in L02 and
  L03 (Nernst, GHK). Figures are mostly structural slide crops with
  hotspots.
- Legacy: L01 and L02 predate the 2026-09-24 authoring rules and are
  in the lint's `LEGACY`; they report but never fail the build.
