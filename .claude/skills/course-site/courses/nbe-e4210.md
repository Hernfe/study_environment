# NBE-E4210 Structure and Operation of the Human Brain

Course-specific section. The course's full rules live in the repo, which
wins over this file:

- `CLAUDE.md`: source hierarchy, reading the material, the lecture build
  workflow, content, writing and design rules.
- `docs/exam-format.md`: the mini-exam format, from Mini Exams 1 to 4
  with solutions or answer keys.
- `docs/scope/nbe-e4210/L0X.md`: per-lecture scope files, with the
  notes-to-deck slide mappings.

Summary:

- Slug: `nbe-e4210`. Aalto University, autumn 2026, running now.
  Instructor Matias Palva.
- 11 lectures (schedule in `src/content/nbe-e4210/registry.js`, from the
  most recent deck). L01 to L06 built.
- Assessment: weekly one-hour paper mini-exams, about two weeks after
  each lecture, one handwritten A4 cheat sheet allowed. Multiple choice,
  scenario multiple choice, circle-the-right-answer inline choices,
  which-statements-are-correct sets (count hidden), classification
  (also as a table of rows by columns), ranking, matching, completion
  and figure labelling with or without a word bank, true or false with
  one-line corrections, direction-of-shift reasoning after a
  concentration change, matching two lists (text or pictures), sorting
  into two or three bins, marking a lesion on a pathway and shading the
  lost visual field. Ten problems per paper, each 1 point divided over
  its sub-items, sometimes unequally. No essays, no calculations.
- Consequences for the site: recap cards are cheat-sheet drafts; the
  lecture quiz follows the mini-exam mix enforced by the lint; medium is
  the paper's ceiling, hard deliberately above it; essays only as
  `beyondExam`.
- Sources in `source/nbe-e4210/`: slides (scope), TA notes reviewed by
  the teacher (order, objectives, self-checks, textbook page filter),
  Bear, Connors and Paradiso 4th ed. in two files (depth; page offsets
  in `CLAUDE.md`), mini-exams with solutions and two pre-2020 essay
  papers (`exams/`), homework (answer style; also scope).
- Profile: conceptual and anatomical, lightly quantitative in L02 and
  L03 (Nernst, GHK). Figures are mostly structural slide crops with
  hotspots.
- L03 and L04 predate Mini Exam 2 and were retrofitted on 2026-09-28
  (inline choice, multiSelect, table classify, concentration-change
  items). They predate Mini Exams 3 and 4 too: `pending` in
  `COURSE_RULES` lists matchPairs and groupItems for both until they
  are retrofitted.
- Homework 3 (`homework/HW3-2.pdf`, chapters 11 to 14) is course
  material for L05 and L06. Its reading guide gives the pages: Ch 11
  pp. 373-386 and 391-393; Ch 12 pp. 417-436; Ch 13 pp. 457-460 and
  469-476; Ch 14 pp. 484-485 (Table 14.1, the strategy, tactics and
  execution hierarchy). The L5 deck (slide 30) says sound localization
  and the vestibular system are not in the exam.
- L05 has no TA notes and none are coming (the lecturer was ill; the
  session was a summary). Its scope file sets scope from the slides,
  the stated page ranges and the TA's exclusions; slides 22 to 39 are a
  TA addendum, in scope but not lectured.
- L06 (chapters 12 to 14) has TA notes written against a deck one slide
  longer from the motor section on (notes slide = ours + 1 from slide 36;
  mapping in its scope file). The page is split into three parts with the
  section `part` field. Its quiz fills exactly the 16-question cap with the
  minimum mix, so it has no diagramAnnotate; lesion logic is practised in
  a clinical case and the pathway-tracer widget.
- Legacy: L01 and L02 predate the 2026-09-24 authoring rules and are
  in the lint's `LEGACY`; they report but never fail the build.
