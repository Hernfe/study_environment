# TU-E4350 quiz format

Source: Week 10 PDF pp. 56–57, citing syllabus v2.1 §7. The first sitting is
26 November 2026, in the second half of the lecture in the usual hall, with
a maximum of 45 minutes and Weeks 1–11 in scope. The alternate is
3 December 2026, adds Week 12, and is for students unable to attend the first.
Attend once. The quiz is individual, in person, closed book and device-free.
It counts for 10% of the grade.

The planned structure is provisional. It comprises 16 single-answer MCQs
(16 points, at least one per week for the first sitting), four 3–4 sentence
framework applications (12 points), and one own-team evidence response
(2 points). The slide qualifies the counts and point values. No later local
blueprint was supplied. Use the actual Client Engagement Log and interview
notes to prepare the team response.

Designated Exam essentials set scope, and backup slides are excluded. The
available W01–W06 decks have no designated essentials. Their learning
outcomes and teaching slides anchor provisional practice. See the local scope
matrix for the source inventory and physical PDF page numbers.

Weekly required practice uses 6–10 single-answer MCQs with 2–4 options,
ordered easy to hard. Use mostly scenario-based decisions. A familiar,
direct match is easy, a comparison or inference is medium, and combining
multiple constraints is hard. This tier split and weekly practice volume
are study design choices. See the task specification for the later mixed
practice quizzes.

Three practice forms use the provisional structure across the available
Weeks 1–10. Weeks 11 and 12 have no supplied source decks. Each practice
form has a 45-minute target and shows its answer key and self-marking
rubric after submission. The optional quick diagnostic is MCQ-only.
