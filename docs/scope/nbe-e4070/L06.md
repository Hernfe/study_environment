## Mathematical and source qualifications

Load-bearing interpretation: feature-row centered matrix F by N; C=Xc Xc transpose/(N−1), C=E D E transpose, scores=E transpose Xc; compact SVD Xc=U S V transpose, r=min(F,N), U F by r, S r by r, V transpose r by N (r may exceed rank); covariance eigenvalue=squared singular value/(N−1); outer-product sum and truncated reconstruction plus mean. With population divisor use N. Correlation requires nonzero SD and consistent divisors; standardized PCA changes scale weighting. These equations support shape/code explanation, no giant formula-drill series.

The p20 E-orientation inconsistency is explicitly qualified using pp13/21. Products on p25 and square-root-eigenvalue shorthand on p21 omit covariance normalization, which is made explicit. Complex Fourier inverse uses conjugate transpose. Repeated covariance eigenvalues can permit other valid PCA eigenbases. Periodic/circulant premise is required, finite estimated covariance is not automatically circulant.
