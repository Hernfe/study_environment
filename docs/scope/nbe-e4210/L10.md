## Source decisions

- The local PDF title and schedule identify L10 as Chapter 23 on 11 November. Slide 3 carries an earlier lecture number in its generic outcomes graphic. The existing 27 November mini-exam date is an estimate from the weekly schedule.
- No L10 teaching-assistant notes or HW5 answer key were located. The textbook offset is printed page minus 465 in part 2. HW5 2(a) belongs to L09. Other Chapter 23 material without a slide or HW5 anchor is outside scope.
- Slides 8–10 use a simplified cleavage and fate model. Preserve the illustrated partition and describe Notch-1 and Numb as upstream regulators. Slide 15 simplifies the disappearance of the transient subplate.
- Slide 37 illustrates selection and pruning at a different scale. Its detail is contextual for activity-dependent refinement. Slide 40 shows infant/preterm electrophysiology, a different recording from the retinal waves on slide 39.
- Slides 47–48 repeat the rodent P25–30 graphic alongside human anatomy. That age label applies to the rodent example. Slides 50–51 describe an animal result. Slides 51–54 show the adult trial, in which improvement under shared conditions did not establish an added fluoxetine effect or isolate the game component.
- Figure extraction reported two linked videos on slide 28 (growth cone attraction and repulsion), one on slide 39 (retinal waves) and one on slide 43 (monocular deprivation). Slides 6, 9, 16, 29 and 31 show frames or illustrations consistent with media use. Slide 39 also links supporting imaging data. No playable media are required or bundled.
- The original diagrams represent qualitative structure, ordering or direction. The shape of the ocular-dominance comparison is schematic and carries no measured values.
