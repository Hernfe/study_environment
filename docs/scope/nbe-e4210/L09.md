## Corrections and emphasis

- Slide 35 gives over 7% annual mood-disorder prevalence, book p. 763 over 9%. Slide 36 reports historical depression figures of 5% annually and 20% lifetime, while p. 763 gives 6% annually for MDD. Keep the source and population/time window attached.
- Slide 19 reproduces an older seven-row grouping that includes OCD and PTSD. The local fourth-edition Table 22.1 (p. 756) lists five disorders, and the adjacent discussion distinguishes OCD and PTSD from that grouping. Treat them as historical table entries and identify the classification being tested.
- Bipolar disorder, dysthymia and schizophrenia appear as background words or cost categories. The deck has no teaching section on them. Book cases, iPSCs, prevalence minutiae and unrelated historical etiologies stay outside the teaching outline. The PET image illustrates binding in the displayed case and does not establish an individual diagnostic test.
- Use original pathway diagrams for p. 24, 25, 28 and 31/41. Put the HPA chain in section 5, feedback in 7, the SSRI mechanism in 8, the hypothesis critique in 11 and the maternal-care results in 13. Media p. 17 and 38 are context. No assessment should require watching them.
