## Exercise audit and exact issues

Exercise5_2026.pdf and Exercise5_answers.pdf each have two pages, read as text and rendered pages. Both problems independently recomputed by scripts/test_l05_sources.py. New practice uses distinct scenarios.

1. PDF7 suggests current injection itself opens channels. Ideal prescribed current into a linear passive membrane does not change conductance; a separately specified synaptic/channel conductance change can lower local resistance and time constant.
2. PDF21 “radial current = 0” means negligible transverse cytoplasmic voltage gradient/isopotential cross-section, not absence of transmembrane capacitive/leak current.
3. PDF22 labels the two-sided factor ri lambda/2 as semi-infinite, while its diameter expression and conductance use the correct one-arm factor ri lambda. Use the latter consistently, cross-check L04 source-arm convention.
4. PDF24 repeats daughter d3111 twice in the final power equation. Correct second daughter is d3112. PDF25 calls parent and daughter dP in prose; daughter is dD.
5. PDF31 calls rho a proportion of current. rho is GD/GS, potentially greater than one; the dendritic fraction of steady total supplied current is rho/(1+rho).
6. Exercise5 answer 2a substitutes d4=4 micrometres although the assignment specifies d4=5. Use the assignment data; the correct equivalent diameter is about 7.42 micrometres, not 6.6. Exact recomputation is in source test evidence.
7. Exercise5 1b is nearly diameter matched, not exactly matched: printed parent 2.15 micrometres is rounded. Unequal soma-to-tip electrotonic paths independently preclude exact reduction. Different soma membrane parameters do not alone prohibit a lumped soma attached to a dendritic cylinder.
8. Exercise5 1c uses a characteristic passive velocity as a transit estimator. Equal estimated arrival is not equality of actual EPSP peak latency or waveform. Changing one branch membrane resistance also breaks the common-material Rall assumptions.
9. PDF18's somatic-only direct recording statement describes the historical modeling motivation, not a universal modern inability to record dendrites.
10. PDF32's common decay refers to the passive model's common asymptotic slow relaxation, not identical entire waveforms, equal local peaks or every component having one decay time. No uncalibrated graph samples are invented.

11. PDF33 prose describes a brief conductance change, but the source input trace is labeled as current. Original axes are retained and this stimulus is used only qualitatively. No conductance values, analytic alpha law or current-to-conductance inference is invented.

## Out of scope and doubts

Detailed HH gating, active channel kinetics, plasticity mechanisms and later recording modes are excluded. HH/GHK are classified only as far as this deck and earlier taught material permit. Asymmetric attenuation appears in the PDF8 agenda but has no dedicated explanation or quantitative panel; no unsupported directional transfer law is introduced. Source traces are existing teaching/model figures, not newly generated experimental or NEURON output. Required derivations are the three-halves rule and algebraic rearrangements above; analytical alpha kinetics is not supplied. No official per-question weights or open-book permission is inferred.
