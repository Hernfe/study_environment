## Source doubts and precise corrections

1. Exercise6 instructions p1 infer the immediate current is purely leak because gating has finite kinetics. A gating variable is continuous, but its current can jump with driving force at fixed nonzero activation. Use their inference only with initially negligible y and after excluding the capacitive transient. Teach the limitation explicitly. Their approximate readings are leak -1nA, fully activated component -2nA, total -3nA.
2. PDF8 says “inward ion flux decreases” under inward rectification without specifying voltage direction. At an inner barrier, making voltage more negative increases inward flux for positive ions. Use the displayed equation, with explicit direction of voltage change.
3. PDF14 writes positive absolute Vm implies depolarization. Depolarization is a change toward a more positive value and need not cross zero; rest-to-less-negative changes also count.
4. PDF15 writes identical symmetric zero-voltage energies while retaining alpha0 and beta0 separately. General baseline asymmetry is allowed in the displayed ratio. The logistic special case requires equal baselines; varying delta alone holds baselines fixed.
5. PDF3 GHK source I/P lacks explicit area normalization. Use current density with standard permeability and concentrations, then specify area separately for total current. Do not mix mM with mol/L in SI substitutions.
6. Source graph readings are approximate: Exercise6 reversal about +30mV, I(-10mV) about -1.2nA, I(+100mV) about +1nA. No more precise source data are claimed. Independent results: 30nS, about 14.3nS, concentration ratio about 0.304. Practice problems use stated distinct idealized values rather than resampling unreadable pixels.
7. Static barrier models describe steady permeation, not absence of physical molecular transition times. The explicit dynamic gating state variable is the distinction in this lecture.
8. C/E/G in group work is not a unique mechanistic identification from any nonlinear curve. Gate-only fits cannot replace a permeation model; full HH equations, channel-specific powers and numerical AP simulation belong to L07 onward.
