## Doubts and corrections

1. Slide 23 prints yinf-(yinf-y0)(1-exp(-kt)), which swaps initial and final limits. Use p25–27's consistent relaxation and teach endpoint verification.
2. Slide 31 labels voltage only as mV. Its empirical coefficients use depolarization from rest, unlike absolute Vm on the preceding plots. Explicitly define U=Vm-Vrest; do not insert a negative absolute resting potential into those unshifted laws. Alpha-n at U=10mV and alpha-m at U=25mV have removable singularities, not zero rates. No empirical temperature scaling is taught here.
3. Slides 7–8 equate injected/measured current with ionic current. Exclude capacitive transients and document positive inward injection balancing positive outward ionic current; amplifier display polarity depends on convention. Leak must be retained or separately corrected in subtraction.
4. Slide 2 asks article-specific stimulation, electrode material and precise amplitudes without supplying the article. The slide excerpt confirms a silver wire within the electrode and about 80–95mV amplitude about 10mm from the cannula, but does not support a complete numerical resting-potential answer. Assess supported apparatus/overshoot/end-injury reasoning and explicitly mark missing article details.
5. Slide 6's no-effect statements are descriptions of its illustrated manipulation, not universal independence of resting voltage from sodium. Group work p33 correctly distinguishes mild potassium elevation from sustained depolarization/inactivation.
6. Slide 24 reverses the gNa/gK order in its summary sentence; use its explicit boxed n^4 and m^3h equations. Gate counts are phenomenological fits, not literal independently hinged molecular gates.
7. No Exercise7 PDF or official solutions are supplied. No simulation or experimental data are generated. New numeric tasks state idealized parameters explicitly and do not claim graph-digitized values. Full HH AP integration/current-balance equation and saltatory propagation belong to L08.
