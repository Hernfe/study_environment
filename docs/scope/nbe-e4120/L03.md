## Doubts and constrained handling
1. Slides use Vm for both absolute membrane voltage and rest-relative response. All cable equations here use V = Vm − Vrest; zero response is not zero absolute membrane voltage.
2. PDF10 says radial current is zero. This is interpreted as negligible radial voltage variation inside the core, not zero transmembrane leak, which would contradict PDF13–15.
3. PDF15/21 use ii for injected current in a term rm ii, although PDF12 defines ii as axial current in amperes. A distributed forcing term must use injected current per length; localized injection is a boundary/jump condition. Teach dimensional distinction explicitly.
4. PDF20 asserts myelin reduces tau. Increasing Rm and decreasing Cm do not determine their product without factors. Teach the asserted trend as conditional and calculate only specified factors; do not claim a universal decrease.
5. PDF22's error-function prose swaps the names, while its integrals and graph identify erf and erfc correctly. Its minus between transient terms is retained: initial and long-time limit checks confirm it. An early audit suspicion of this minus was rejected after testing both limits; no erroneous plus is published.
6. PDF23 captions a temporal charging expression as a steady-state infinite-cable solution. Keep temporal charging distinct from spatial steady attenuation.
7. Exercise 3 and its solution are absent. No assignment-specific claims or software procedure are certified. Finite ends, Rall branching, HH and detailed saltatory mechanisms belong to later decks.
