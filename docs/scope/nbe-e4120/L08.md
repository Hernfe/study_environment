## Doubts and corrections
1. 8:1 p4 drops driving forces in its last propagation equation. Retain every g(V−E) term. The source a denotes radius and Ri denotes bulk axial resistivity; its full speed prefactor K lacks a complete dimensional definition, so assess scaling rather than absolute prediction from that K.
2. 8:1 p5 omits leak in a parenthetical ionic-current definition. Ionic/capacitive balance includes leak unless explicitly neglected. Space clamp removes spatial variation, not temporal changes; voltage clamp also fixes voltage.
3. 8:1 p9 says depolarization activates h. Availability h falls with inactivation while n rises. Positive momentary peak slope is only fast-subsystem stability, not a permanent full-system equilibrium. Conductance comparison alone does not determine current sign (p12); driving forces matter.
4. 8:1 p17 prints odds/(1−odds). Normalization requires odds/(1+odds). The low-activation exponential is conditional; effective charges near 6 and 5 are slope estimates, not literal sensor counts.
5. 8:2 p6 says myelin usually decreases τ. Increased R and decreased C alone do not guarantee this: ideal identical layers in series leave RC unchanged. Teach the product and distinguish longer λ from a guaranteed faster τ.
6. 8:2 p8 illustrative L=1mm,D=10µm,d=7µm is schematic, not an exact instance of both empirical fits. Use fit labels and distinguish inner d from outer D.
7. Exercise8 #1 maxima are maxima of the supplied 10mV grid only. No unique continuous peak is specified without interpolation. #2 uses absolute voltage, unlike L07 rest-relative empirical rates. #3 assigns charge displacement per channel but gives only measured conductance at −10mV, not confirmed maximal activation. The estimate g/D equals γ only if fully open; otherwise it is γPopen, a lower bound on γ for positive open probability. No official worked solution supplied.
8. 8:2 p12 anesthetic node counts and p16 optical/periaxonal numbers describe the supplied experiments, not universal blocking cutoffs or universal waveform differences. Never claim voltage vanishes at internodes.
