## Doubts and exclusions

1. PDF3–4 mixes fixed-volume redistribution with a water-flow approximation keeping potassium effectively fixed. Teach the sequence qualitatively; do not certify its x≈1.8 as an exact closed-system solution.
2. PDF23/26 weighted voltage requires zero net applied/transmembrane current as well as zero voltage derivative. An AP peak alone only makes capacitive current zero. State the additional condition.
3. PDF7 calls the GHK result current but permeability times molar concentration produces current density unless area is included. Use SI current density and explicitly state uniform-patch area if converting to current.
4. Exercise2 P2 potassium inference assumes comparable potassium conductance at matched voltage/time; amplitude alone cannot distinguish changed conductance from changed reversal. Include that limitation.
5. PDF11 charge is a magnitude; signed inner charge is negative at negative voltage. Distinguish charging increment from preexisting signed charge. The claimed fraction of all cellular ions lacks a specified volume/concentration and is not recalculated as universal.
6. Unavailable textbook GHK derivation is not invented or required. HH gating equations and cable theory belong to later lectures and are excluded.
