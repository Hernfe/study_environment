## Source doubts and qualifications

1. 9:1 p9 calls transitions instantaneous and gradients not dissipated. Teach unresolved on the experimental time scale and approximately unchanged concentrations for brief activity, not literally zero transition time or zero energetic cost.
2. 9:1 p14 places ATP-sensitive channels under two-TM examples while p17 uses six-TM voltage channels. Teach pore-forming inward-rectifier topology separately from regulatory subunits. Do not claim every member of a loosely named category has identical topology. Evolution diagrams are the source's schematic lineage, not proof of a universal chronology.
3. 9:1 p16 peptide occlusion is a taught inactivation model, not a universal molecular mechanism for every voltage-gated channel. S4 movement and ionic permeation carry different currents. TRP statements and thermal thresholds are family/example generalizations.
4. 9:2 p2 ambiguously associates no functional change with altered gating/conductance/selectivity/signaling. Distinguish neutral variants from function-changing variants. A mutation alone does not establish a disease mechanism.
5. 9:2 p4 toxin occurrence, lethal-dose and food-preparation anecdotes are not assessed as clinical/toxicological guidance. The subtype table is used to contrast relative TTX sensitivity, not a universal threshold or absolute immunity.
6. 9:2 p6 clinical-development phase and safety wording are dated slide claims, not established current clinical status. No dosage or present-day treatment recommendation is given. 9:2 p7 and p11 atrial selectivity and class effects are conditional mechanisms, not guarantees of safety or uniform effects on all cardiac tissue.
7. 9:2 p12 QT >480 ms is the source's simplified threshold, not a diagnostic rule independent of rate, patient, or protocol. No QT-correction formula is supplied. hERG inhibition is a hazard signal, not sufficient alone to quantify clinical risk.
8. 9:2 p13 normalized hERG curves and tail current preserve source axes and protocol. Qualitative reading only; no precise digitized IC50/Hill fit is claimed. Tail recovery from inactivation is distinguished from slower deactivation.
