# L10 scope

The 37-page Sensory receptors and synaptic transmission deck and exam information pp2–4 define the exam path.

| Section | Deck pages | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| transduction | 2–8 | Receptor classes, direct and indirect transduction, graded potentials, rod example |
| synapses | 9–16 | Gap junctions, direction and delay, chemical and ribbon transmission |
| vesicles | 15–21 | Calcium entry, fusion, transmitter criteria and recycling |
| receptors | 22–27 | Ionotropic/metabotropic signaling, NMDA, driving force and shunting |
| clamp | 25–29 | Baseline subtraction, synaptic and baseline slopes, apparent reversal, space clamp |
| quanta | 24, 30–32 | NMJ, mEPP/EPP, Katz argument and assumptions |
| distributions | 32–35 | Binomial, Poisson, expected trial counts and variance |
| estimates | 36–37 | Direct, failure and CV estimators, frequency comparison |
